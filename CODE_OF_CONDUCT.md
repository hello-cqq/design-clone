# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, condensed)

Based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (CC BY 4.0). The full text applies; this file summarizes the parts we enforce.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Empathy, kindness, and respect for differing viewpoints.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — including about clones: critique the artifact and the evidence, not the person.
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes, and learning from them (this repo keeps a public `docs/LESSONS.md` for exactly that).
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language/imagery, sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment; publishing others' private information without permission.
- **Shipping or demanding infringing material**: passing cloned third-party UI/assets off as original, or asking maintainers to bundle copyrighted assets, logos, fonts or captures you do not have rights to.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope & enforcement

Applies in all community spaces and when representing the project. Report to the maintainers via the channel in `SECURITY.md`/repo profile. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate. Decisions are documented privately and communicated to the reporter.
